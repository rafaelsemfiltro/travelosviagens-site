Privacy Policy

TravelOS Viagens designs tailor-made trips and assists you through our website, email and WhatsApp. Here we explain what personal data we collect, what we use it for, who we share it with and how you can exercise your rights, under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018, LGPD).

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Who is responsible for your data

TravelOS Viagens is the trade name of RAFAEL VERSUTTI NOETZOLD, registered under CNPJ 57.944.150/0001-70, a travel agency registered with Cadastur and run by Rafael and Leonardo. It decides how your data is used (controller).

For any privacy matter, which is also our data protection officer channel, contact us at rafael@travelosviagens.com.br or on WhatsApp at +52 999 152 7265.

2. What data we collect

Data you give us. When you talk to us we receive your name, phone, email, desired destination, travel dates, number of travelers and children's ages, budget range and travel preferences. On WhatsApp we also receive your number, your profile name and the content of the messages and audios you send.

Booking data. When a purchase moves forward, we ask for what is needed to book and issue, such as each traveler's full name, date of birth and passport number, through a secure channel agreed with our team. Children's ages and data are provided by the guardian and used only to plan and book the trip.

Browsing data (only if you accept). If you accept the cookie notice, we use Google Analytics to measure site use: pages visited, clicks on WhatsApp buttons, device type, language and approximate location (city and country). This is statistical and does not tell us who you are.

Please do not send card numbers, passwords or documents through the WhatsApp chat. The site has no sign-up forms and does not create accounts, and contact happens through WhatsApp.

3. What we use it for and legal basis

We use the data to answer your messages, understand the trip you want, prepare quotes and itineraries, make bookings, issue the contract, follow your trip and provide support. The legal bases are performance of a contract and pre-contractual steps at your request (art. 7, V, LGPD), compliance with legal and tax obligations (art. 7, II) and, for support and security, legitimate interest (art. 7, IX).

We only send news and offers if you agree to receive them (consent, art. 7, I), and you can ask us to stop at any time by replying STOP.

We use browsing data only to understand which pages and buttons work and to improve the site, based on your consent, which you give in the cookie notice and can withdraw whenever you like.

4. Support with an artificial intelligence assistant

On WhatsApp, the first contact is handled by Tavi, a virtual assistant powered by artificial intelligence. It asks questions to understand your trip and organizes the information for our team. Tavi does not confirm prices, availability or bookings, and does not replace human support: whenever you ask, the conversation is handed to Rafael or Leonardo.

To generate replies and transcribe audio, messages and audios are processed by automation and artificial intelligence technology providers that we hire.

5. Who we share it with

We share your data only with those needed for your trip to happen: airlines, hotels, operators, transfer and tour companies and insurers, each with its own privacy policy.

We also use technology providers that run our support: WhatsApp (Meta), data hosting, automation and artificial intelligence processing, plus Google (Analytics), the latter only if you accept cookies. We may share data with authorities when the law requires it.

We do not sell your data and do not use it to send third-party advertising.

6. Transfers outside Brazil

Some of these providers, such as Meta, Google and hosting and artificial intelligence services, may process data on servers outside Brazil. When that happens, we follow art. 33 of the LGPD and use providers with compatible protection measures.

7. Cookies

When you enter the site we show a cookie notice. If you decline, Google Analytics is not loaded and no measurement cookie is stored. If you accept, Google Analytics stores cookies to count visits.

The site also keeps on your device, without cookies, your language choice and your answer to the cookie notice. During your visit, it also keeps, only in that tab, which package you viewed last, so your WhatsApp message arrives with that information. It disappears when you close the tab.

To change your choice at any time, click here to see the cookie notice again.

8. How long we keep it

Conversations with people who did not book are kept for up to 12 months. Data of those who booked is kept for up to 5 years, to meet legal and tax obligations and to defend rights. After that, data is deleted or anonymized.

Browsing data is kept for the period configured in Google Analytics, which is short.

9. Your rights

Under the LGPD you can ask at any time for: confirmation that we process your data; access to it; correction of incomplete or wrong data; anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data; portability; information about who we share it with; and withdrawal of consent. Write to rafael@travelosviagens.com.br. We reply within 15 days.

If you believe your data was not handled properly, you can also complain to Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

10. How to request deletion of your data

Send an email to rafael@travelosviagens.com.br with the subject "Data deletion", stating the WhatsApp number used in the conversations. We confirm receipt and delete your data within 15 days, except what the law requires us to keep. You can also send STOP on WhatsApp to stop receiving messages.

11. Security

We use restricted access, passwords, secure connections and reputable providers to protect the information. No system is completely immune to failures, and in case of an incident that may bring relevant risk or harm, we notify you and the ANPD, as the law requires.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The update date appears at the top of the page and, for a relevant change, we notify you on the site.
