To add to your itinerary

Destinations to extend your trip.

Places we know up close that pair well with a few extra days on your Caribbean trip. Several can also be done as a day trip from Cancún. Open a destination to see the details.

21.16°N 86.85°W

Cancún

Zona Hotelera, where we live — a firsthand recommendation.

The Zona Hotelera is where we live and experience the Mexican Caribbean day to day. Besides the turquoise-water beaches, it's the gateway to almost every excursion and destination on this itinerary — which is why we know every resort, restaurant and the quietest times to visit up close.

20.63°N 87.07°W

Playa del Carmen

Urban beach, nightlife and Fifth Avenue, in the heart of the Riviera Maya.

About 45 minutes from Cancún, Playa del Carmen has a more urban, lively vibe, with Fifth Avenue packed with shops, bars and restaurants just steps from the beach. It's a great stop for mixing resort days with livelier nights, and it also works as a base for trips to Cozumel and Tulum.

20.21°N 87.46°W

Tulum

Ruins, cenotes and the most photographed town in the Mexican Caribbean.

Seaside Maya ruins, cenotes throughout the region and a town known for its bohemian-chic scene — Tulum blends history, nature and a food scene that has grown a lot in recent years. It's about 1h30 from Cancún, and you can visit for the day or stay a few nights.

21.23°N 86.73°W

Isla Mujeres

A quick crossing, calm beaches and island pace.

A 30-minute boat ride from Cancún, it's a small, unhurried island with calm beaches like Playa Norte and a charming town center to explore by golf cart. It's a great option both for a day trip and for anyone who wants to swap the resort for a few island days.

20.42°N 86.92°W

Cozumel

A world reference for diving and coral reefs.

A world reference for diving, with the second-largest coral reef on the planet right at its doorstep. Beyond the reefs, it has quiet beaches and a pleasant port town to walk around between dives — reached by ferry from Playa del Carmen.

21.52°N 87.38°W

Holbox

A car-free island, pale sand and glowing bioluminescent nights.

A car-free island with sandy streets, colorful houses and nights when the sea glows with bioluminescence (in the right season). It's more secluded and low-key than the rest of the Riviera Maya, ideal for anyone looking to truly disconnect.

18.68°N 88.40°W

Bacalar

The lagoon of seven colors, between the Caribbean and the jungle.

Known as the lagoon of seven colors, it sits inland in Quintana Roo, between the Caribbean and the jungle. The clear freshwater looks like a giant natural pool, and the town's pace is much slower than the coast.

20.5°N 87.4°W

Cenotes

Natural freshwater pools hidden in the Quintana Roo jungle.

Natural freshwater pools hidden in the jungle, formed by underground rivers — some open-air, others inside caves. You can swim, snorkel or even try cenote diving, and several are just minutes from Cancún or along the way to Tulum.
